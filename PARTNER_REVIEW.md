# Pairlo partner review

Last updated: 2026-11-09 (Asia/Singapore). This is a walkthrough; completing the document is not evidence that a partner has tested the app.

## Before starting

Use a desktop browser and the final review URL supplied by the owner. The current provider URL is https://pairlo-poc.vercel.app; Vercel access protection may appear before Pairlo sign-in. The GoDaddy hostname and partner account are pending. Use the account/password handled privately by the owner. Public signup and self-service recovery are unavailable.

A separate account starts with an empty private workspace. The owner's saved fixtures are not shared. Use clearly marked synthetic examples; the app records actions carried out elsewhere and does not send candidate files/messages, issue invoices or move money.

## Suggested walkthrough

1. Create a labelled sample company and enter its fee percentage. Add a business contact, then open or reuse that person's candidate profile. Keep confidential candidate notes/pay in the candidate view; check that the contact view shows shared identity and business context only.
2. Add a second candidate if useful. Upload a synthetic PDF CV under CV & files, reload and download it. Keep real personal data out of review screenshots and feedback.
3. Create a permanent role for the company, choose its hiring contact and enter an optional percentage override. Find/link a candidate. Open the same application from the role and candidate, and check that its response, stage and discussed pay belong to that one opportunity.
4. Log a conversation. If several roles are linked, record separate outcomes and select only the tasks wanted. General candidate expectations change only when explicitly selected. Open its source links, then complete/reopen a task and check Today using Hong Kong dates.
5. Record a shortlist already sent outside Pairlo. Record client feedback, interview arrangements and an offer decision on the application. An accepted latest offer can create a placement. Enter an explicit fee basis/currency or fixed fee, and optional guarantee dates/terms.
6. Record one sample invoice and partial payments performed outside Pairlo. Check the outstanding balance. If testing a payment-entry correction, enter a reason and check retained history. A guarantee check-in/task must stay independent of invoice/payment status.
7. Download one of the Excel templates. Read Instructions, keep all headers and use text-format phone numbers. Import companies before dependent relationships. Check a few valid/invalid/duplicate rows, confirm selected eligible rows, reload the saved results and open the canonical records. Existing saved fields should stay unchanged. Limit each file to 100 data rows / 1 MB.
8. Search workspace by name, formatted phone and record type. Review Analytics with client/date filters and its metric definitions; currencies stay separate and recorded stage reach does not infer missing history. Save personal defaults and check a new draft, then restore the defaults wanted.
9. Edit a sample record without saving, open a related preview and attempt navigation. Keep editing should retain the draft; save/discard should follow the chosen action. Reload saved records, then sign out and check that a protected link returns to sign-in.

## Record feedback

Add each issue to BACKLOG.md with a stable ID and include:

- Page/record URL, desktop browser and time in Hong Kong.
- The action, expected behavior and actual result; say whether a reload changes it.
- A redacted screenshot and the shortest steps needed to reproduce it.
- Whether work is blocked, data was lost, or the issue is an improvement.

The owner decides product/UX changes. Accepted significant decisions are appended to DECISIONS.md; implementation and actual verification are updated in PROJECT_STATUS.md/TESTING.md. Do not mark final acceptance until the partner has completed the needed persisted journeys on the intended HTTPS domain.
